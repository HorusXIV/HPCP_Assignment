    15                                               where 
    16                                           
    17                                                   g=K.DEM

    19                                               Regularized approach solves this via
    20                                             
    21                                                   ||K.DEM-g||^2 + lamb ||L.DEM||^2=min

    30                                           
    31                                                   DEM_lamb = Sum_i (sva_i/(sva_i^2+svb_i^1*lamb)) * (g.u) w
    32
